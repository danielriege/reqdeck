# Tick

A to-do app for iPhone and iPad that gets out of the way. Open it, type, done.

**For whom.** Someone who thinks of ten things on the way to work and forgets nine of them.
Shared household lists come second.

**What it does.** Lists of tasks with an optional due date, a reminder when one is due,
the same tasks on every device through the user's own iCloud, and what is due today on the
home screen.

**What it is not.** A project manager. No priorities, tags, sub-tasks, or a server of its
own — the data lives on the device and in the user's iCloud, and nowhere else.

**Success.** Capturing a task is faster than opening Notes.
