# Open Questions

Everything not yet decided. One question per `###` heading, fields on single lines.

```
### <ID> — <short title>
**Question:** <what has to be decided>
**Answer:** <blank until decided>
**Blocks:** <what is waiting on it, or —>
```

IDs are `OQ-TTNN` — `TT` is the topic number, `NN` the question within it. To add a
question, take the next free `NN` in that topic. **Questions never move.** When one is
answered, fill in `Answer:` and leave it where it is.
